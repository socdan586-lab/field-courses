# Field and Profession Courses

> Understand a field by learning how its work really fits together.

## The Opportunity

Field-focused learning experiences explain the people, ideas, and neighboring disciplines that shape real work—helping learners see more than a job title or course outline.

## Who It Serves

Learners exploring a profession, educators, and professionals broadening their perspective.

## What It Makes Possible

- Introduce the context and practices of professional fields
- Show how disciplines contribute to real-world systems
- Connect learning to meaningful project examples

## How People Use It

1. Choose a field related to your goals.
2. Explore its core ideas and relationships with other fields.
3. Apply what you learn through a practical activity or project.

## How It Connects

- Links CareerWBT field exploration to EruditeWBT courses and projects.
